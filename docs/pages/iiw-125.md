6.2.4 Formulae for Stress Intensity Factors

Stress intensity factor formulae may be taken from literature, see references [4-1 to 4-8]. For the majority of cases, the formulae given below are sufficient.

Tab. {6.2}-2: Stress intensity factors at welds

Surface cracks under membrane stress

The formula for the stress intensity factor K1 is valid for a/c < 1, for more details see ref. [4- 2]

K1 = F /(BAa / Q)  A Fs

Q  = 1 + 1.464 (a/c)1.65

Fs = [M1 + M2A(a/t)2 + M3A(a/t)4]AgAfAfw

M1 = 1.13 - 0.09 (a/c) M2 = -0.54 + 0.89 / (0.2 + a/c) M3 = 0.5 - 1 / (0.65 + a/c) + 14 (1 - a/c)24

fw = [sec(BAc /(a/t) /(2Ab))]  1/2

g and f are dependent to direction

```text
           "a"-direction: g = 1          f = 1
           "c"-direction: g = 1 + [0.1 + 0.35 (a/t)2]
               f = /(a/c)
```
