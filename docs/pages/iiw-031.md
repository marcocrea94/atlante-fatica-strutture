Type “b” hot spots:

The stress distribution is not dependent of plate thickness. So, the reference points are given at absolute distances from the weld toe, or from the weld end if the weld does not continue around the end of the attached plate.

```text
  4)   Fine mesh with element length of not more than 4 mm at the hot spot: Evaluation of
       nodal stresses at three reference points 4 mm, 8 mm and 12 mm and quadratic
        extrapolation (eq. 4).
```
