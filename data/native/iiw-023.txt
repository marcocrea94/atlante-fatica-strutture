The secondary bending stress caused by axial or angular misalignment needs to be
 considered if the misalignment exceeds the amount which is already covered by fatigue
 resistance S-N curves for the structural detail. This is done by the application of an additional
 stress raising factor km,eff (see 3.8.2). Intentional misalignment (e.g.allowable misalignment
 specified in the design stage) is considered when assessing the fatigue actions (stress) by
 multiplying by the factor. If it is non-intentional, it is regarded as a weld imperfection which
 affects the fatigue resistance and has to be considered by dividing the fatigue resistance
 (stress) by the factor.
